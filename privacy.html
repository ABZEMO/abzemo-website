Privacy Policy

We respect the privacy of people who interact with ABZEMO and aim to handle information responsibly.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ABZEMO may collect, use, and protect information when you visit or interact with the ABZEMO website.

ABZEMO is committed to responsible information handling and to maintaining appropriate safeguards for information entrusted to us.

2. Information You May Provide

Depending on how you interact with the website, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, organization, email address, business requirements, or other information included in a message sent to us.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information provided to ABZEMO may be used to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requests.
  • Understand business and project requirements.
  • Provide information about relevant ABZEMO services.
  • Communicate regarding potential business relationships.
  • Improve our website, services, and business processes.

4. Website Communications

If you contact ABZEMO through an email address or other communication channel provided on this website, the information contained in that communication may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and manage the relevant business relationship.

5. Cookies and Analytics

The current ABZEMO website may not use cookies or third-party analytics services. If analytics, cookies, tracking technologies, or other third-party services are introduced in the future, this policy may be updated accordingly.

6. Third-Party Services

ABZEMO may use third-party technology providers in the future to support website hosting, communication, analytics, artificial intelligence, automation, or other business functions.

Where applicable, information handled through such services may also be subject to the relevant provider's terms and privacy policies.

7. Data Security

ABZEMO aims to use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was provided, legitimate business requirements, legal obligations, or dispute resolution.

9. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law and circumstances, you may have rights relating to information held about you, including requesting access, correction, or deletion.

Requests may be sent to: info@abzemo.com

10. Changes to This Policy

ABZEMO may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the website, services, technology, or legal requirements evolve. The updated version will be published on this page.
