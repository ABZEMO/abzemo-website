# Compliance Agent Workflow

## Continuous compliance flow

1. **Define scope** — identify entity, jurisdiction, framework, policy, process, control and effective date.
2. **Collect evidence** — retrieve approved records from connected systems and identify missing evidence.
3. **Normalize** — map source records, controls and requirements to a common compliance model.
4. **Evaluate** — test requirements and controls using configured rules, thresholds and effective dates.
5. **Detect** — create exceptions, violations, missing-evidence items and control failures with source traceability.
6. **Classify risk** — apply configured severity, likelihood, materiality and escalation criteria.
7. **Recommend** — prepare remediation, CAPA, owner assignment, due dates and required approvals.
8. **Approve** — pause for required human approval before filings, attestations, risk acceptance, permission changes or other consequential actions.
9. **Execute** — perform only permitted actions through configured connectors.
10. **Verify** — confirm external-system response, resulting state and evidence.
11. **Monitor** — continue scheduled checks, regulatory-change monitoring, deadlines, remediation and management reporting.

## Controls

- Requirements and policies are versioned by jurisdiction, framework and effective date.
- Evidence must remain traceable to its source and observation time.
- High-risk exceptions are escalated according to configured policy.
- Segregation-of-duties and access controls are checked where authoritative data is available.
- Consequential compliance actions are approval-gated by default.
- External writes and filings require configured credentials and permissions.
- Every consequential action and assessment must have an audit record and verification result.
