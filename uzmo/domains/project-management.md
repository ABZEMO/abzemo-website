# Project Management Domain

## Coverage
- Initiation, chartering and business case
- Scope, requirements and WBS
- Schedule, milestones and dependencies
- Cost, estimating and budget control
- Resource and capacity planning
- Risk, issue, assumption and dependency management
- Quality and acceptance
- Stakeholder and communications management
- Procurement and vendor coordination
- Change and configuration control
- Benefits, outcomes and value tracking
- Governance, PMO and portfolio interfaces
- Reporting, lessons learned and closure

## Delivery approaches
UZMO should evaluate context before selecting:
- Predictive
- Adaptive
- Hybrid

The selection is a decision aid, not a hard-coded winner. PMI describes predictive, adaptive and hybrid approaches as appropriate for different project conditions. The current PMBOK Guide (8th edition) also expands practical guidance while retaining support for these approaches.

## Agent responsibilities
The Project Management Agent can:
1. understand the objective and constraints;
2. recommend a delivery approach;
3. generate a project structure;
4. create work items, milestones, dependencies and controls;
5. monitor progress and exceptions;
6. trigger risk/issue/change workflows;
7. produce project and executive reporting;
8. verify completion criteria before closure.

Reference sources: PMI standards and guides; official Scrum Guide; official Kanban Guide.
