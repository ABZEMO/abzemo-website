# UZMO Healthcare Domain

UZMO Healthcare is an enterprise healthcare orchestration domain for hospitals, clinics, health systems, home-care providers, laboratories and digital-health organizations.

## Core capabilities
- Patient intake, registration, triage support and appointment coordination
- Clinical workflow coordination and care-team task orchestration
- Patient journey, care-plan and follow-up management
- Clinical documentation, summarization and information retrieval
- Medical records and longitudinal patient-context workflows
- Remote patient monitoring and home-care coordination
- Preventive-care, chronic-care and population-health workflows
- Bed, room, staff, equipment and resource coordination
- Pharmacy, laboratory, imaging and referral workflow coordination
- Prior authorization, coding, billing and revenue-cycle workflow support
- Patient communication through approved chat, voice, SMS and portal channels
- Quality, safety, incident, audit and accreditation readiness
- Infection-control and clinical-operations workflow support
- Healthcare supply, inventory and procurement coordination
- Analytics, capacity planning, utilization and operational intelligence
- Healthcare knowledge management and clinician education support

## Specialist agent model
- Healthcare Orchestrator Agent
- Patient Care Coordinator Agent
- Clinical Documentation Agent
- Remote Monitoring Agent
- Hospital Operations Agent
- Revenue Cycle Agent
- Patient Engagement Agent
- Quality & Compliance Agent
- Healthcare Intelligence Agent

## Safety and governance
UZMO may summarize, coordinate, retrieve information, detect workflow exceptions and recommend actions. Clinical diagnosis, treatment decisions, medication decisions, patient discharge decisions and other regulated/high-impact clinical actions remain subject to appropriate qualified-human review and organization-specific controls. All healthcare deployments should enforce least-privilege access, auditability, privacy, consent, provenance, escalation and model-validation controls.

Healthcare agentic AI is an active 2026 deployment area spanning clinical support, administration, patient engagement, remote monitoring and hospital operations. Current research emphasizes safety, controllability, governance, interoperability and human oversight.
