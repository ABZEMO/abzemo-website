# UZMO Compliance Domain

The Compliance Agent is the specialist governance and continuous-control capability inside UZMO. It evaluates configured regulatory requirements, internal policies, controls and evidence across connected enterprise systems without assuming a single jurisdiction, framework or software platform.

## Scope

- Regulatory and legal compliance monitoring
- Policy, SOP and control testing
- Regulatory-change monitoring and impact assessment
- Licenses, permits and certifications
- Audit preparation, evidence collection and finding remediation
- Compliance calendars, deadlines, alerts and escalations
- Incidents, violations, breaches and CAPA
- Regulatory reporting and filing support
- AML/KYC, privacy, cybersecurity and third-party controls
- Procurement, finance, accounting, HR/labour, tax and customs compliance
- Environmental, health and safety compliance
- Document retention and evidence management
- Training, acknowledgements and compliance attestations
- Segregation of duties, access controls and conflict-of-interest checks
- Whistleblowing and compliance-case workflows
- Continuous monitoring across approved ERP, CRM, HRMS, databases, spreadsheets and documents

## Control model

UZMO may collect evidence, compare records against configured requirements, identify exceptions, classify risk, prepare remediation and draft reports automatically. Consequential actions remain approval-gated by default, including regulatory filings, formal attestations, risk acceptance, external regulatory communications, controlled permission changes and material legal/financial commitments.

## Jurisdiction and framework model

Compliance rules must be deployment-configured and versioned by jurisdiction, industry, entity, regulatory framework and effective date. UZMO should distinguish a current requirement from historical or superseded requirements and preserve the source/evidence used for every assessment. It must not present a researched interpretation as an authoritative legal determination.

## Integration model

The agent can be routed to approved ERP, finance, procurement, HRMS, CRM, document, spreadsheet, identity/access, security and web-research tools. Credentials, write permissions, regulatory sources and client-specific policies are supplied by deployment configuration. UZMO must not claim an external filing, remediation or permission change succeeded unless the connected system verifies the resulting state.

## Example requests

- "Check this month's HR records for configured labour-compliance exceptions."
- "Monitor our licenses and certifications and alert owners before expiry."
- "Compare this procurement transaction against our approval and segregation-of-duties policy."
- "Review the new regulation and identify which configured controls may be affected."
- "Prepare an audit evidence pack for the selected controls."
