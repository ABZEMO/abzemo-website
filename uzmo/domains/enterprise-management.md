# UZMO Enterprise Management Domain

UZMO Enterprise Management is the cross-functional management layer for organizations, groups, business units and multi-company enterprises. It connects strategy, governance, finance, people, projects, operations, sales, risk and technology into one governed management model.

## Core capabilities
- Enterprise strategy, objectives, OKRs, KPIs and execution tracking
- Corporate planning, annual planning and rolling forecasts
- Business-unit, subsidiary, branch and multi-company management
- Organization structures, authorities, delegations and decision rights
- Executive dashboards, management reporting and board reporting
- Portfolio, program and project governance
- Enterprise performance management and scenario analysis
- Finance, budgeting, cost control and management accounting coordination
- Sales, revenue, customer and commercial performance management
- Workforce, talent, capacity and organizational planning
- Operations, supply chain, procurement and service management
- Enterprise risk management, controls, compliance and audit readiness
- Policy, SOP, approval and exception management
- Vendor, contract and third-party management
- Business continuity, resilience and crisis-management workflows
- Enterprise architecture, applications, data and technology portfolio governance
- Transformation, change management and benefits realization
- Sustainability, ESG and enterprise-impact reporting
- Cross-functional workflow orchestration and management-by-exception
- Enterprise knowledge, decision history and institutional memory

## Operating model
UZMO should act as a management intelligence and orchestration layer over existing ERP, CRM, HRMS, project, finance, supply-chain and other systems rather than requiring the client to replace them. Agents should operate through approved APIs/tools while respecting existing permissions, business rules and audit trails.

## Governance
High-impact corporate decisions, financial commitments, policy exceptions, employment decisions and other controlled actions require configurable human approval. Enterprise deployments should enforce RBAC/ABAC, segregation of duties, audit logs, data lineage, tenant isolation, approval policies and continuous monitoring.
