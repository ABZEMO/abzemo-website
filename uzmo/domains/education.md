# UZMO Education & Learning Domain

UZMO Education supports schools, colleges, universities, vocational institutes, training providers, edtech organizations and corporate learning teams.

## Capabilities
- Student onboarding, admissions and records workflows
- Course, program, timetable and academic planning
- Learning management and personalized learning paths
- AI tutoring, guided practice and feedback
- Assessment generation and grading assistance
- Curriculum mapping and learning-outcome alignment
- Teacher/instructor planning and classroom support
- Attendance, engagement and intervention workflows
- Student advising and support workflows
- Faculty workload, scheduling and performance workflows
- Research administration and academic knowledge management
- Accreditation, quality assurance and compliance preparation
- Fees, scholarships, procurement and administration workflows
- Career services, internships and employer coordination
- Learning analytics, retention and early-warning intelligence
- Training, certification and corporate learning management

## Specialist agents
- Education Orchestrator Agent
- Admissions Agent
- Student Success Agent
- AI Tutor Agent
- Assessment Agent
- Faculty Agent
- Academic Operations Agent
- Research & Knowledge Agent
- Education Intelligence Agent

## Governance
UZMO can personalize learning, coordinate workflows and assist educators. High-impact decisions involving student discipline, safeguarding, admissions, progression or other sensitive determinations require institutional policy, appropriate human review, privacy controls and auditability.
