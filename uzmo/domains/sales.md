# Sales & Revenue Domain

UZMO sales is an agentic revenue system, not just a CRM.

## Revenue lifecycle
- ICP definition and segmentation
- Territory/account planning
- Market and account research
- Prospect discovery
- Contact and buying-committee mapping
- Lead enrichment and deduplication
- Lead scoring and qualification
- Intent and buying-signal monitoring
- Personalized multi-channel outreach
- Meeting booking and discovery
- Opportunity creation and stage progression
- Pipeline management
- Deal health and risk detection
- Proposal, quotation and approval workflows
- Negotiation support
- Contract handoff
- Closing and won/lost analysis
- Customer handover and expansion
- Renewal, upsell and cross-sell

## Sales intelligence
- ICP and persona analysis
- Account intelligence
- Competitor intelligence
- Buyer intent
- Next-best-action recommendations
- Deal scoring
- Pipeline coverage
- Forecasting and scenario analysis
- Stalled-deal detection
- Win/loss analysis
- Revenue attribution
- Sales performance analytics

## Sales methodologies and frameworks
- Consultative selling
- Solution selling
- Value selling
- SPIN Selling
- Challenger Sale
- MEDDICC / MEDDPICC
- BANT
- GPCT / qualification frameworks
- Account-Based Selling / ABM
- Inbound and outbound sales
- Land-and-expand
- Cross-sell / upsell
- Customer success / renewal motions

Frameworks are selectable according to the organization's configured sales playbook; UZMO must not invent qualification criteria when a company has defined its own.

## Agentic guardrails
Agents may research, qualify, draft, schedule, update CRM and recommend actions autonomously within permissions. Pricing exceptions, contractual commitments, final negotiation positions and other high-impact actions require configurable human approval.
