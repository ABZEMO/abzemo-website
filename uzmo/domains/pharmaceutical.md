# UZMO Pharmaceutical & Life Sciences Domain

UZMO Pharmaceutical supports pharmaceutical, biotechnology, clinical-research, CRO, CDMO and life-sciences organizations across R&D, quality, regulatory, supply chain and commercial operations.

## Capabilities
- Drug-discovery research and scientific literature intelligence
- Target, mechanism, compound and evidence knowledge workflows
- Preclinical and clinical-trial planning and coordination
- Site/patient recruitment workflow support
- Clinical documentation and study-data workflows
- Pharmacovigilance and safety-case workflow support
- Regulatory intelligence, submissions and document control
- GxP-aware quality, deviation and CAPA workflows
- Manufacturing, laboratory, batch-documentation and exception workflows
- Supply chain, inventory, cold-chain and distribution monitoring
- Demand forecasting, product availability and market intelligence
- Medical affairs, scientific content and field-force workflows
- Market access, pricing, reimbursement and competitive intelligence
- Patent/literature monitoring and product-lifecycle intelligence

## Specialist agents
- Pharma Orchestrator Agent
- Drug Discovery Intelligence Agent
- Clinical Trials Agent
- Pharmacovigilance Agent
- Regulatory Affairs Agent
- Quality/GxP Agent
- Manufacturing & Supply Agent
- Medical Affairs Agent
- Pharma Commercial Intelligence Agent

## Governance
UZMO supports research, evidence synthesis, workflow execution, documentation and recommendations. Regulated scientific, clinical, manufacturing, safety and regulatory decisions require the applicable qualified-human approval, validation, traceability and compliance controls.
